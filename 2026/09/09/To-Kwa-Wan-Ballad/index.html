To Kwa Wan Ballad

Written by My Little Airport Ah P originally

This rainy night,
do you have spare time now?
I am at To Kwa Wan at the moment,
thinking about what to do with my future,
My only wish is that the station on SC line
can have a delayed opening.
I cannot afford any increase in my rent.

This rainy night,
Am I still in your heart?
Haven’t been working for ages,
why don’t my worries recede?
My only wish is that days can pass in trivial ways,
and I know it is the most difficult.
May you and I stay in the darkness for one more moment.

############################
############################

This rainy night,
I am lying on my sofa, occasionally falling asleep.
The radio says that North Korea has launched missiles.
The other side of the earth has now vanished.
Everything is not virtual enough,
since reality hasn’t reached the bottom.
Desperation comes first, then one can see hopes on earth.

There will be a day when you and I are no longer romantic,
having work we dislike but have to do.
Will it remind you how adorably you have smiled?
