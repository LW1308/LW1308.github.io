Unwritten acknowledgements

Due to many different reasons, the section of acknowledgements printed could not fully deliver my thankfulness to all contributors in the past stage. Even if none of them will eventually find this article, I still feel it’s a must to make such a supplement to them. Even though I want to keep their anonymity on this very personal but publicly searchable page, I still believe that I can send my sincere gratitude for them.

First of all, thanks to all my friends within my neighborhood these two years. To be honest, days with them felt like a grand illusion in my path. Most of the time it was super enjoyable. Thanks for their company, their help when I felt angry or terrible, their care and understanding, and the new knowledge they delivered to me. These friends are truly fantastic amazing people and I was so blessed to be neighbor with them for a short or a longer time. Probably I don’t always speak it out loud but I do love you all.

Second, I would like to say thank you to the night club I worked in most of Fridays in this year. I have spent so many wonderful nights with incredible colleagues. We club people are smart, brave, kind, talented, and responsible. Club shifts were tough but we could always manage to make it run properly. Specifically I want to thank for the appreciation my colleagues and friends there gave me. I feel very warm and honored to know that my efforts and contributions were seen. I will never regret working there. I will not forget those nights we worked together. En gång klubbutskott – alltid klubbutskott. It is love!

There are also many important people who have helped me in the past few years. My language teachers are nice people. Some old friends keep giving me mental support and company. Several nice people showed their patience when I felt totally lost, in different dimensions. Thank you all.

In the end, thanks for all podcasts, series, films, songs and music that kept me accompanied in countless lonely days and nights. Unfortunately, for me a large portion of time has to be consumed by myself. These sounds confort me through my headphones.
