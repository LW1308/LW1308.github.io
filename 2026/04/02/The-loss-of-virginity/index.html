The loss of 'virginity'

This is an article, together with its title, that I have promised to write. The virginity here is not related the intimacy.

The whole idea about this title/article essentially began at my high school time. Back then I got a neighborhood which was not all time enjoyable. A bunch of old or semi- old people gathered together everyday and kept gossiping loudly. One regular sunny day while waiting for my father parking our car, I overheard their knowledgable gossiping accidentally. The exact topic is quite hazy to recall. But surely they were talking about the division line between boy and man.

This pluralistic question can be answered or interpreted differently with various cultural and contextual background. However, I was at the end of my adolescence and therefore way too attracted and influenced by those teenagers’ erotic night talks in my dormitory and sexually explicit descriptions in those Japanese literature. So while I was overhearing, the answer in my mind was naturally the loss of virginity. The moment a boy losses his virginity, he will officially become a man, like the moment the custom officer stamps on your document, ka-ta, you are officially outside the country.

For my neighborhood most of them actually gave the same answer. It propably could become the general consensus in the block. However, one man(probably) gave a different answer, which left me a longer impression. He said:

*** “The moment a boy touches a gun.” ***

In the coming years I read books and watch films gradually, then I begin to know some art theory. The idea of guns(especially pistols) function as phallic symbols has a long history and significant influence. In academic scenario, such as psychoanalytic and cultural criticism, it is an active line of thought. In this line, the gun is not simply treated as a visual analogue of penis, but as a signifier of penetration, control, threat, and potency. It gives a symbolic order. For example, in classical Hollywood films, if a man pulls out his pistol and points at another guy, it is to say I got your ass now so from now on listen to me. A very clear hierarchical structure is constructed here. Sounds very masculine right? If you got a gun you would become a man. It is of course not to simply say ahha now I know gun = penis. If in the same film, with very intense music, the same man pulls out his penis or a dildo, pointing it to the other guy, it might not be that masculine. The film will typically turn itself into a comedy or something else. What is important here is that guns are often linked to the logic of the phallus or at least, typically domination. From Thelma & Louise (1991) to Bugonia (2025), the link between masculinity and gun keeps contributing to and influence the artworks. To be fair it is quite unusual to hear such an academic answer in such a gossiping.

Anyway, after hearing this, my adolescent wet dreams were still there unchanged. All those libidinal fantasies, images in my adolescent mind were still thriving. Simultaneously I also became interested in shooting. Just like the real virginity, I wanted to have all things happened under control in a safe way. This fantasy was realized last month, many years after the adolescence.

According to Tripadvisor’s lists it seems like shooting ranges are most popular entertainment areas in Poland, with affordable prices and English services. I took a short trip to Poland with only two targets, pierogi and pistol.

I would like to say the shooting range I visited was very responsible and safe. The experience was actually much better than I expected. Since I had never touched a gun before and I didn’t even know how to say thanks in Polish, I was quite nervous in the beginning. But with the patient instructor at the range and the reliable CZ Shadow 2, I felt enjoyable, reliefing, and quite satisfied in the end.

The interesting part is before I stepped into the range, I was about to ask the silly question are these weapons real or kinda toys. But the moment I touched the pistol, its weight and texture answered my question already. It’s truly instinctive, by looking at the gloss on the metal a man (yeah finally, under my old neighbor’s standard) could feel it should be authentic, even if it’s his first time. To look into this topic slightly deeper, in some sense it is quite logical and reasonable for guns to have attractive and beautiful appearance. Most guns are designed by men and I suppose they have no questions about making the phallus prettier. In some sense it is also good to make them beautiful, since it implies how dangerous or lethal they are. Colorful frogs, mushrooms and many other beautiful natural things are poisonous.

Anyway, the trip is short but it leads to a quite long writing. The time spent on this writing is surely way longer than the time in the range. But it leads to a nice thinking process. Probably this is the true way of studying. Reading within a classroom may not be sufficient.
